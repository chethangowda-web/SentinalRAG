Customer Service Guide

1. Contact Channels
Support is available by phone, email and live chat. Critical incidents are handled 24/7. All other requests are handled Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 UTC.

2. Languages
Support is offered in English, Spanish and German.

3. Ticket Handling
Every ticket is acknowledged within 15 minutes of submission. Refund and billing requests are routed to the billing team through the support portal.

4. Escalation
Tickets start at Tier 1 support. A ticket that is unresolved after 24 hours is escalated to Tier 2. A ticket that is unresolved after 72 hours is escalated to the support manager.

5. Customer Satisfaction
After a ticket is closed the customer receives a satisfaction survey. Scores below 3 out of 5 trigger a follow-up call from a team lead.
