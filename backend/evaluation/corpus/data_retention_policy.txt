Data Retention and Security Policy (2025)

1. Retention Periods
System and access logs are retained for 90 days. Audit logs are retained for 1 year. Daily backups are retained for 35 days.

2. Account Deletion
When an account is deleted, all of its data is purged within 30 days.

3. Encryption
Customer data is encrypted with AES-256 at rest and protected with TLS 1.3 in transit.
