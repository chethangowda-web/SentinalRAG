Premium Service Addendum

This addendum applies only to customers who have purchased the Premium plan. It adds to, and for Premium customers replaces, the terms of the Standard Service Level Agreement.

1. Availability
The uptime guarantee for Premium services is 99.99%, measured on a monthly basis.

2. Incident Response Times (Premium Plan)
Critical incidents receive a first response within 15 minutes. High priority incidents receive a first response within 1 hour.

3. Dedicated Support
Every Premium customer is assigned a named account manager and a 24/7 priority support line.

4. Service Credits
If monthly uptime falls below 99.99% but is at least 99.9%, the customer receives a service credit of 20% of that month's fees. If monthly uptime falls below 99.9%, the customer receives a service credit of 50% of that month's fees.
