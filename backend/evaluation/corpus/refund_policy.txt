Refund Policy

1. Satisfaction Guarantee
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase by contacting customer support. The refund will be processed within 5-7 business days after approval. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

2. Non-Refundable Items
Custom hardware orders and setup fees are not refundable once the service has been activated.

3. Annual Plans
An annual plan that is cancelled after the 30-day guarantee period receives a pro-rated refund for the unused full months, minus a 15% administration fee.

4. How to Request a Refund
Open a ticket in the support portal under the category Billing, then Refund. Include the order number and the reason for the request.
