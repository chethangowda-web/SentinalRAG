Network Services Service Level Agreement - Standard Plan

1. Availability
The minimum uptime requirement for network services on the Standard plan is 99.9%, measured on a monthly basis. Uptime is calculated as the total minutes in the month minus minutes of unplanned downtime, divided by the total minutes in the month.

2. Performance Targets
Round-trip latency within a single region must stay below 50 milliseconds. Packet loss must stay below 0.1% averaged over the month.

3. Scheduled Maintenance
Planned maintenance takes place every Sunday between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC. Customers receive at least 72 hours of notice before any maintenance window. Time spent in announced maintenance windows does not count as downtime.

4. Incident Response Times (Standard Plan)
Critical incidents receive a first response within 1 hour. High priority incidents receive a first response within 4 hours. Normal priority incidents receive a first response within 1 business day.

5. Service Credits
If monthly uptime falls below 99.9% but is at least 99.0%, the customer receives a service credit of 10% of that month's network fees. If monthly uptime falls below 99.0%, the customer receives a service credit of 25% of that month's network fees. Credits must be claimed within 30 days of the end of the affected month by opening a billing ticket. Credits are applied to the next invoice and are not paid out in cash.

6. Exclusions
Downtime caused by force majeure events, by the customer's own equipment or configuration changes, or by third-party services outside our network is excluded from uptime calculations.
